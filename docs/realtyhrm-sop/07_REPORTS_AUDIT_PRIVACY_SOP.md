# Reports, Audit and Privacy SOP

Use Reports screens only for the scope granted by the server. Prefer canonical attendance/report RPCs and record the report date range, filters and export actor. Review audit history for attendance, permissions, field evidence, tickets and configuration changes.

Do not place OTPs, access/refresh tokens, service keys, webhook secrets, raw employee GPS trails or unnecessary personal data in SOPs, tickets or screenshots. Exported reports must be stored in approved locations with access limited to the intended HR/admin scope.
